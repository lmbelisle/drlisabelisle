Taking Stock Assessment

Is this still
the work, and
what’s next?

You trained for one version of a medical career. Years in, many physicians ask a harder question: is this still the work I want, and if not, what would I build instead?

Taking stock is not burning out or walking away. It is the ordinary, periodic work of checking whether your career still fits the person you have become, and deciding your next move. Most physicians never make the time, and drift instead.

Answer the questions below and you’ll get an honest read: your current profile, the research behind it and one concrete next step. It takes about five minutes.
