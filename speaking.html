About Lisa

Lisa Belisle, MD, PhD, MPH, MBA, is a physician executive with separate board certifications in Family Medicine and in Preventive Medicine, and a Certificate of Added Qualification in Health Care Administration, Leadership and Management. She is a Certified Physician Executive (CPE) with the American Association for Physician Leadership and a Fellow of the American Academy of Family Physicians (FAAFP). She has led health systems through virtual care transformation, workforce redesign, and organizational change as an Associate Chief Medical Officer. Her doctoral research on leading through change in healthcare delivery settings has been published in the Healthcare Administration Leadership & Management Journal, and her leadership programs for women in medicine have earned CME accreditation through the Maine Medical Education Trust. Based on the coast of Maine, she works nationally as a physician leadership speaker, coach, and consultant, and hosts Radio Maine.
