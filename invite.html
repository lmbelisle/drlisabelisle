Looking for a keynote, fireside, workshop, or retreat? Here’s what Lisa speaks on, where she’s been, and how to reach her.

Lisa has presented at national medical conferences and cultural gatherings on the intersections of medicine, creativity, leadership, and seasonal wellbeing. Her talks draw on more than three decades of clinical experience, training in Chinese medicine and Five Phase theory, and the stories that emerge when medicine and art share a room. She brings the ear of a clinician, the pace of a broadcaster, and the attention of a writer.
