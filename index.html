MD  ·  PhD  ·  MPH  ·  MBA
Physician  ·  Writer  ·  Host  ·  Teacher

Lisa Belisle works at the intersection of medicine and the human spirit: in the clinic, on the air, on the page, and in conversation with artists, healers, and thinkers who believe that how we live matters as much as how long we live. She is the host of Radio Maine, the creator of The Bountiful Path, and a board-certified family physician whose practice integrates Western medicine with acupuncture and Five Phase theory. She lives and works off the coast of Maine.
