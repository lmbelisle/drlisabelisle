Privacy

Privacy
Policy.

This policy covers the client-onboarding tool used by Dr. Lisa Belisle’s coaching and consulting practice, and explains how it accesses and uses data from Google accounts.

Effective October 3, 2026

What the tool is

The tool is a private, single-operator program used by Dr. Belisle to set up coaching clients. For each client it creates a folder, a working document, and an intake form inside Dr. Belisle’s own Google account, and shares the client-facing items with that specific client. It is not a public product and is not offered to other people to install or use.

Google account access

The tool accesses one Google account — Dr. Belisle’s own practice account — using Google’s official APIs. With that access it:

  • creates and organizes folders, documents, and forms used to onboard a client, and
  • shares specific items with the individual client the workspace is for.

It reads the signed-in account’s email address once, solely to confirm it is operating on the correct account before doing anything.

How the information is used

Google account access is used only to create and organize these coaching materials. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to onboarding a client.

Storage

The tool runs on Dr. Belisle’s own computer. Its authorization credential is stored locally on that machine and is not transmitted anywhere else, published, or shared.

Sharing and transfer

Google account data accessed by the tool is not sold, rented, or transferred to any third party. The only sharing that occurs is giving an individual coaching client access to the specific workspace created for them.

Limited use

The tool’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Dr. Lisa Belisle at lbelisle@littlejohnisland.me.
