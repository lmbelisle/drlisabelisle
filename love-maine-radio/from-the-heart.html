Episode summary

Dr. Emily Isaacson, artistic director of the Oratorio Chorale and the Maine Chamber Ensemble, and psychologist Dr. Mary Plouffe, author of the memoir I Know It in My Heart, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about following the heart through music and through grief. Isaacson recalled the concert that made her commit to a life in music and described founding the Portland Bach Festival with faculty from the Juilliard School. Plouffe shared the story behind her memoir, written after the death of her young niece, and the unforgettable moment a verbal four year old put words to her own dying. Together with Dr. Lisa Belisle they considered how artistic vocation and clinical practice both ask people to stay present to what cannot be planned. The conversation moved through choral music, childhood grief, family memory, and the experiences that push us beyond the daily, drawing a quiet line between the conductor on the podium and the psychologist at the bedside, both attending to what most needs to be witnessed in a given moment.
