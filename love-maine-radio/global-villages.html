Episode summary

Adam Burk, a Portland-based community organizer involved with TEDxDirigo, and Dr. Anouar Majid, scholar of global cultures and dialogue, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle and co-host Genevieve Morgan on Love Maine Radio for a Labor Day conversation about how local gatherings build something larger than themselves. Burk reflected on the role of technology as connective tissue between in-person events, and on the moment that magic happens when people set their phones down and meet face to face. Majid described conversation itself as the most radical act available to us, a medium for staying connected and alive that opens up rather than closes down. Together they considered how Maine's small-state intimacy can model a wider kind of village, the difficulty of holding open-ended conversations across difference, and the relationships that childhood connection seeds into adult well-being. Dr. Belisle framed Love Maine Radio itself as one small attempt at building such a global village.
