Episode summary

Alice Chaplick and Dr. Matthew Siegel, a psychiatrist at Spring Harbor Hospital, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about autism and the families living alongside it. Chaplick, a mother of three who works with Marcy Booth at Booth Maine, spoke about her youngest son Aiden, diagnosed with autism at eighteen months, and the years of early intervention and advocacy that followed. She described positive improvements in his health and well-being and the persistence it took to find them. Siegel described his clinical work and the vision behind a new program at Spring Harbor designed so that families feel met, understood, and at home. The conversation moved across the autism spectrum, the milder presentations that often go unrecognized at school and at work, the persistence required to find services in Maine, and the ways community and professional care intersect when a family is facing something hard.
