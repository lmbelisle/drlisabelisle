Episode summary

Christina McAnuff, executive director of the Olympia Snowe Women's Leadership Institute, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about a three-year program that builds the confidence, aspirations, and leadership skills of girls across Maine. McAnuff explained that the institute, founded in 2014, welcomed its first class of sophomores in fall 2015 and has since grown from seven schools in Androscoggin County to a statewide reach of 36 schools across all 16 Maine counties. She described the three-year track of My Values, My Voice, and My Vision, which moves students from self-knowledge through listening and speaking into resilience, reframing failure, empathy, and gratitude. She traced her own twisty path from leading the high school abroad program at the Council on International Educational Exchange. The conversation moved through girls' leadership, public schools, and the steady work of building a stronger next generation of Maine women, with McAnuff describing the program as a place where students learn to listen as carefully as they speak.
