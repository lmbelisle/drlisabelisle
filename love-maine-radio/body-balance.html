Episode summary

Julie Wright, owner and director of Soma Massage and Wellness in South Portland, and Carrie Levine, certified nurse-midwife and founder of Whole Woman Health in Newcastle, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about helping clients find balance. Wright, who has been practicing massage since 2003, described her work in therapeutic and nurturing massage, stress reduction, and grief recovery, and reflected on the rising interest in acupuncture, naturopathy, and energy work as people look beyond primary care for support. Levine, a functional medicine specialist and longtime advocate for women in labor, described her early hospital advocacy on behalf of laboring patients and the work she now does at Whole Woman Health. From massage, midwifery, and functional medicine to dietary change, energy work, and the integration of conventional and complementary care, the conversation considered how Mainers can support their bodies through every season of life in a Maine practice room.
