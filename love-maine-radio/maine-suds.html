Episode summary

Fred Forsley, founder and president of Shipyard Brewing Company, and Cyndi Prince, founder of LooHoo, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about Maine entrepreneurs building lasting businesses. Forsley reflected on Shipyard's growth into the largest brewery in the state and a national leader in the craft brew industry, on his many ventures in food, hospitality, and real estate, and on the changing shape of Portland's waterfront and downtown housing stock. Prince described how she built LooHoo, the Maine-based maker of reusable wool dryer balls, by leaning into a positive story about the product rather than a negative one about its alternatives. From brewing and craft sponsorship of community road races to small-batch manufacturing, household sustainability, branding instincts, and the values that shape a Maine company, the conversation considered what it means to grow a business with deep roots in a place in a small but ambitious state.
