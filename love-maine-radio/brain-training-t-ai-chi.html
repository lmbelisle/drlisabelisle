Episode summary

Counselor and educator Pamela Blodgett and Ken Ryan, founder of Maine Coast T'ai Chi Chuan, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about cultivating wellness through brain and body practices. Blodgett, board certified in neurofeedback, described how parents seeking help for a child with attention difficulties can find qualified practitioners through the Biofeedback Certification International Alliance, and the careful work of building optimal patterns through biofeedback and neurofeedback. Ryan, who instructs students in t'ai chi and qigong, reflected on how Chinese medicine understands organ systems and meridians, and on the way particular movements activate them, a practice with thousands of years of history behind it. From neurofeedback and clinical certification to t'ai chi, qigong, and the slow learning of an internal art, the conversation considered what it takes to bring brain and body into closer relationship in a small Maine studio and a quiet living room.
