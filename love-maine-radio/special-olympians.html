Episode summary

Raf Adams, owner of SoPo SUP Shop in South Portland, and Cri Swift, partner at the law firm Sidley Austin, parents of Special Olympian Henry, along with Karen Johnson, parent of a past Special Olympian and coach and coordinator of the Special Olympics team in Cape Elizabeth, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about children, athletics, and the quiet awe of daily life. Adams reflected on how raising Henry, who has Down syndrome, has made him more compassionate and more aware that everyone carries something. Swift described the family's resources and the options Henry and his brother Dylan will have as he grows. Johnson spoke about reaching across school districts to build connections among children who will one day live and work side by side as adults. The conversation considered parenting, travel, the Sochi Olympic Games, neighborhood, and the way Special Olympians ask the adults around them to see differently.
