Kids, Community & Coffee #280

Episode summary

Katie Wallace and Katie Brown of The Locker Project, alongside Bard Coffee co-founder Bob Garver, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about responding to community need. Wallace, the founder and president of The Locker Project, and Brown, its executive director, described the program's work with southern Maine schools to provide students with classroom snacks and take-home food, an idea that grew from the small acts of teachers who quietly stocked their lobbies with what hungry students needed. Garver reflected on the early years of Bard Coffee, when organic and fair-trade sourcing was still uncommon and Portland customers helped make the business possible. From school hunger and educator advocacy to ethical sourcing, small-business persistence, neighborhood loyalty, and the values that shape a coffee company, the conversation considered how communities take care of their own across southern Maine neighborhoods across the year, one act at a time.
