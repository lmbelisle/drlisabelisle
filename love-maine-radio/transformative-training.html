LOVE MAINE RADIO · EPISODE 210 · SEPTEMBER 25, 2015

Transformative Training #210

Episode summary

Linda Banks, interior designer and owner of Simply Home in Falmouth, and Dr. Zev Myerowitz Jr., a chiropractor and acupuncturist, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio to discuss the impact of triathlon training on the body and the wider shape of a life. Banks recounted how a difficult professional and personal stretch and an invitation to join the Tri for a Cure team, sponsored by Maine Media Collective, set her on a path that took off forty pounds and brought her into multi sport endurance racing. She described training as a practice of intention rather than competition. Myerowitz brought a clinician's view to the conversation, considering how strong, flexible bodies support emotional and social well being. Together they reflected on physical change as inevitable, on the role of community in athletic life, and on the way that bodily training can shift mood, work, and the everyday experience of being a person in the world.
