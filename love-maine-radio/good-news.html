Episode summary

Natural Foodie columnist Avery Kamila of the Portland Press Herald and Sunrise Guide publisher Heather Chandler joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about treading lightly on the planet through everyday choices. Kamila described the column she had built around Maine's natural foods scene, from local farmers and bakers to the small producers stocking co-op shelves. Chandler shared the work of the Sunrise Guide, the long-running directory of Maine's earth-friendly businesses, products, and services. Dr. Belisle framed the show through her interest in what makes people happy, citing a University of Vermont Twitter study that placed Maine among the happiest states in the country, alongside Hawaii, Nevada, Utah, and Vermont. Together they considered local food, sustainable household goods, journalism that lifts up good news, and the small consumer choices that quietly add up to a healthier Maine. Dr. Belisle reflected on the accumulating evidence that an appreciative approach to ordinary life is itself a form of medicine.
