Episode summary

The Reverend Kit Sherrill, long-serving summer rector of the Chapel of All Saints by the Sea in Southport, and Al Moses, the chapel's caretaker and a descendant of its founders, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about Maine's summer chapels and the people who keep them. Sherrill described a small island sanctuary where Protestants, Catholics, Jews, and Muslims have sat together in the same bench, and where guests show up who do not attend church the rest of the year. Moses traced the chapel's roots to a minister from Gardiner who came down to picnic with friends in the 1860s and to his own great-grandfather, who held services under oak trees and inside the house he built long before the chapel itself was raised. Together they considered worship, hospitality, and continuity. The conversation moved through architecture, family memory, and the kind of welcome a summer chapel offers.
