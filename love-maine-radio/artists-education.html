Episode summary

Suzette McAvoy, director of the Center for Maine Contemporary Art in Rockport, and Roger Dell, director of education at the Farnsworth Art Museum in Rockland, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about the place of art in Maine life and how it reaches people of every background. McAvoy reflected on the steady supply of artists connected with Maine, and the way new artists continue to emerge regardless of how many have already been shown. Dell spoke about the importance of art teachers in Maine schools and the irony of a state that prizes creative workers without always supporting the educators who model creativity, innovation, and imagination for children. Dr. Belisle shared her own path from giving up on art in eighth grade to picking up a camera years later. The conversation considered classrooms, museums, the early classmate who could draw Snoopy and the rest of the Peanuts gang, observation, and the everyday eye.
