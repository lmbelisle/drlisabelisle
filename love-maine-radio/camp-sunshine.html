Episode summary

Michael Katz, executive director of Camp Sunshine, and Nancy Cincotta, the camp's psychosocial director, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio along with Meg Dermody, a mother of a Camp Sunshine family, to discuss more than three decades of work with families navigating life threatening childhood illness. Katz, who has been with the camp since its founding and also serves on the boards of Maine Summer Camps and the Maine Camp Experience, described how the camp brings the whole family, the ill child, the well siblings, and both parents, into a week of retreat and respite. Cincotta, who came to the work after thirty years at Mount Sinai, where she remains on faculty, spoke about the psychosocial program that supports parents through community, resource sharing, and brief release from the daily weight of a child's illness. Dermody offered the family view from inside the experience. The conversation honored the camp's thirty one years of welcoming families from around the country to Maine.
