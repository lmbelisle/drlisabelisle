Episode summary

Dr. Linc Avery, orthopedic surgeon and division leader of Sports Medicine at Maine Medical Partners Orthopedics and Sports Medicine, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio along with Dr. Kirsten Buchanan and Matt Kraft of the University of New England Physical Therapy Department to discuss the care and prevention of knee injuries. Avery described the everyday cases that walk into his clinic, including middle aged patients trying to start jogging on knees that have worn early, and the work of getting people back to the activities they love. Buchanan and Kraft spoke from a physical therapy and research perspective, with attention to ACL injury prevention, particularly in young female athletes. The conversation reached across surgery, rehabilitation, conditioning, and the long path between a teenage ACL tear and an active adult life. Together the three considered how the knee, a hinge joint caught between feet and hips, can be supported through training, modification, and the right partnerships in care.
