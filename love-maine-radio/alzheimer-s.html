Episode summary

Best-selling author and former physician Dr. Tess Gerritsen and Leon Duff, volunteer and board member with the Hospice of the Waterville Area, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about Alzheimer's disease and what it asks of families. Gerritsen, author of the Rizzoli and Isles series, spoke openly about her father's early-onset Alzheimer's, his confusion and rapid decline in San Diego, and her own fear of a disease that touches so many older Americans. She traced the dark thread that runs through her fiction back to a childhood spent at horror films with her mother. Duff reflected on the absence of a solution and the work of moving through a process while hoping for a meaningful ending. The conversation considered what it means to lose one's mind, to become unrecognizable to loved ones, and to draw on a writer's or volunteer's life as ground from which to face such loss.
