LOVE MAINE RADIO · JUNE 1, 2018

Dr. Owen Logue

Episode summary

Dr. Owen Logue, executive director of the Governor Baxter School for the Deaf at the Maine Educational Center for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing on Mackworth Island in Falmouth, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about more than thirty years in education and a life shaped by his own deafness. Logue, who was born deaf in Orono, described the effort his parents made to create speech, hearing, and summer-program opportunities for him in an era when no laws required public schools to enroll deaf children. He learned the full story only at twenty-one, when he asked his mother, and it redirected his life toward making others' lives better. He moved from social work into special education through a federally funded program and went on to teach deaf students at Bangor High. The conversation moved through family, sign language, public education, and the long stewardship of a school that has held generations of Maine children.
