Episode summary

Kate Gawler, a nurse in women's health with Maine Family Planning in Augusta, and Roy Bouchard, retired lakes biologist with the Maine Department of Environmental Protection, family members of Sue Goller, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio along with Ron Hoffman of Compassionate Care ALS for a conversation about amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. Gawler, Sue's sister, spoke about her sister's response to the disease and the wide circle of people Sue continues to inspire each day. Bouchard, Sue's husband and main caregiver, described the quiet that has settled over their household as the family misses her everyday voice. Hoffman reflected on the willingness to step into the fire of difficult feeling, to attend to the shadow, the darkness, and the uncertainties. The conversation drew on Dr. Belisle's May Maine Magazine piece about artist John Imber, who lived with ALS, and considered what the disease asks of families, friends, and communities.
