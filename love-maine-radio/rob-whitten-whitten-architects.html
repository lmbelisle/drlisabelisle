Episode summary

Rob Whitten, founder of Whitten Architects, a residential architecture firm based in Portland, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio alongside landscape architect Todd Richardson, owner of Richardson and Associates in Saco, to talk about the collaboration between landscape and building design. Whitten described looking for a landscape architect whose work complemented his residential practice and who understood the Maine vernacular and what gives the state its sense of place. The two had collaborated for more than a decade on projects that integrated house and site from the first sketches in the studio. Richardson explained that landscape architects designed landscapes the way architects designed buildings, carrying the work through construction drawings and construction administration. The conversation moved through residential design, the partnership between the two firms, the value of bringing the landscape architect in early, and the shared craft of building places that felt native to the Maine coast.
