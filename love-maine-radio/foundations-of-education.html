Episode summary

Kate Snyder, executive director of the Portland Education Foundation, and Gabe Weiss, president of the Yarmouth Education Foundation, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about community-led support for public schools. Snyder described how research into education foundations around the country shaped Portland's independent nonprofit, which raises philanthropic support for the city's public schools. Weiss reflected on the wide range of projects Yarmouth's foundation had funded, from iPads in early elementary classrooms to artists in residence at the high school and a shellfish upweller installed at the town landing where students study clam growth before reintroducing the clams into the local flats. From philanthropy and school finance to enrichment programming, the relationship between districts and donors, and the civic muscle of a town that decides to invest in its children, the conversation considered how communities strengthen public education for the next generation of Maine students in two southern Maine towns.
