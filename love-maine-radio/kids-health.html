Episode summary

Dr. Lynne Tetreault of Maine Medical Partners Pediatrics in Saco and medical director of Vax Maine Kids, Dr. Amy Belisle of the Maine Quality Counts Child Health Quality Improvement Project, Dr. Lisa Letourneau, executive director of Maine Quality Counts, and Jen Rohde, Staci Olson, and Sandi Sinclair of the Maine chapter of Girls on the Run joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a far-ranging conversation about children's health. Tetreault discussed the protective math of immunization rates and the risks that emerge when coverage falls. Lisa Letourneau spoke about preventing the chronic conditions that follow children into adult life and how active kids translate into healthier adults at lower cost. The Girls on the Run team described running as a vehicle for self-worth, framing the program as a gift of perspective rather than a selfish endeavor. Dr. Belisle drew on her years at MaineHealth and Raising Readers to frame the show.
