Episode summary

Deborah Walters and Jane Gallagher of Safe Passage, along with Space Gallery executive director Nat May, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio to consider what it takes to keep good works going. Walters, a board member at Safe Passage, was preparing to kayak from Maine to Guatemala to raise funds for a school serving children and families who live near the Guatemala City garbage dump, an organization founded by Dr. Lisa Belisle's Bowdoin College classmate Hanley Denning. Gallagher, a program manager with Deedle Partners and co-chair of the Casco Bay Friends of Safe Passage, reflected on five trips to Guatemala and a family that sponsors two Guatemalan students. May spoke about the work of Space Gallery in Portland and the steady project of holding open an experimental arts venue. The conversation moved across nonprofit evolution, lasting impact, and how organizations stay nimble as the needs around them shift over time.
