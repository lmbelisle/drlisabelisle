High-Quality High Schools #302

Episode summary

Daniel Marchetti, head of school at Hebron Academy, and Luke Shorty, executive director of the Maine School of Science and Mathematics in Limestone, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about what makes a strong Maine high school. Marchetti, who came to Hebron in 2016 after leading the Grammar School in Putney, Vermont, and serving in multiple positions at the Hillside School in Massachusetts, described growing up inside a boarding school and the work of reimagining education without losing the people who built it. Shorty, a mathematician by training, talked about the appeal of teaching a room of thirty engaged teenagers and the beauty he wanted to pass on. Together they discussed boarding life, charter schools, residential community, and the long view of preparing young Mainers for what comes next. The conversation moved through tradition, innovation, and the steady work of teaching, with both leaders attentive to the question of how a Maine secondary school keeps its character while also preparing students for a world that keeps changing around it.
