"With my 41st birthday coming up this January, I'm having to take on some new fears. And one of these is being a radio host." — Lily, monologue close (Jan 1, 2012)

Episode summary

On the New Year's Day episode of Love Maine Radio, Dr. Lisa Belisle turned the microphone around, joined by co-host Genevieve Morgan along with John McCain, Courtney Ogden, and Leanne Ouimet. Morgan, who had first profiled Dr. Belisle for Maine Magazine's inaugural Wellness issue, interviewed her about the origins of the show and the intention behind a talk radio program offering meaningful mind, body, and spirit connections with a global perspective. Past guests from the program's first fifteen weeks returned briefly to speak about their own intentions for the year ahead. The conversation also explored the program's affiliation with Maine Magazine and the Give Back ethos that runs through the show. Dr. Belisle framed health and wellness as a shared responsibility, calling listeners stakeholders alongside doctors and nurses, and inviting them into the work of moving past fear of the unknown to learn that the body has wisdom, that life has wisdom, and that the universe has wisdom worth listening for.
