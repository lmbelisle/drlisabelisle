Episode summary

Andrea Graichen, vocal soloist and board member of the Choral Arts Society of Maine, and William Kirkpatrick, Program Director for the Alzheimer's Association Maine Chapter, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio to discuss the connection between music and memory. The conversation centered on the Choral Arts Society's New England premiere of Robert Cohen's Alzheimer's Stories, presented in collaboration with the Alzheimer's Association and conducted by Robert Russell. Graichen described how a chance encounter in a coffee shop led to the inclusion of poet Judy Prescott, whose book Searching reflected on her mother's experience with the disease. Kirkpatrick spoke about how music remains one of the last connections people retain near the end of life, and how it reaches memory, joy, and relationship when other paths have closed. Together they reflected on the long preparation behind the program, the role of music therapy in dementia care, and the partnerships in Maine that made the project possible.
