Economies of Scale, #108

"It's easier to leverage a good idea in Maine. That's the value of smallness." — Philip Conkling, Island Institute

Episode summary

Philip Conkling, founder of the Island Institute and contributing editor at Maine Magazine, and Shannon Kinney, founder and chief client success officer of Dream Local Digital, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about entrepreneurship at the scale of Maine. Conkling described what he calls the value of smallness, the way a good idea can move quickly through a Maine network in ways that would be harder to achieve in Silicon Valley or Los Angeles. Kinney spoke about working with businesses across the country and bringing best practices home to Midcoast roofers and other small Maine companies as far afield as Bakersfield, California, or Kenosha, Wisconsin. They considered the entrepreneurial spirit in a state of farmers, fishermen, mill workers, and river guides, the fierce independence and quiet interdependence that runs through Maine work, the supportive role of the Maine Media Collective, and the value of learning skills that no formal degree had taught them.
