Episode summary

Joan Benoit Samuelson, winner of the first Women's Olympic Marathon in 1984 and founder of the TD Beach to Beacon 10K, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio along with volunteer coordinator Maya Cohen and her husband Dr. Mylan Cohen, who staffs the medical tent. Samuelson, who grew up running the roads of Cape Elizabeth, described founding the race to bring an international field to her hometown and to benefit Maine children's charities, and she reflected on health beginning with the youngest among us. Maya Cohen detailed the volunteer operation behind the race, including composting, cardboard recycling, shuttles, and a bike valet. Dr. Mylan Cohen spoke about caring for runners who arrive at the medical tent. Together they marked the twentieth running of the race and the community that sustains it. The conversation moved through Olympic history, hometown roads, public health, and the volunteer culture that makes a major race possible.
