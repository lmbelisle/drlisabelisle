"Maine… part of an international trade ecosystem that in fact we could be at the center of." — Philip Conkling, on East-West Highway

Episode summary

Philip Conkling, contributing editor at Maine Magazine and author of books on the Gulf of Maine, and Peter Vigue, chairman and chief executive officer of the Cianbro Companies, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about the future of business in Maine. Vigue described a state in the middle of an economic transformation and his work advocating for an east-west highway using I-95 as a corridor for energy transport, as well as Maine's entry into offshore wind development. Conkling drew on his article A Leader Like No Other in the May issue of Maine Magazine, and reflected on what it would mean for Maine to see itself not at the end of the road but at the center of an international trade ecosystem. Together they considered workplace wellness, Cianbro's strong safety record under Vigue's leadership, and the long task of using Maine's resources and ingenuity while honoring the state's character.
