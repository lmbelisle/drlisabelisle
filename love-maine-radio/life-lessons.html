Episode summary

Bates College president Clayton Spencer and Derek Pierce, founding principal of Casco Bay High School in Portland, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio to consider how learning happens at every age and what makes a school worth attending. Spencer, who came to Bates in 2012 after fifteen years at Harvard University, described the work of helping students ask the larger questions of life, not how to write a resume but what gives satisfaction and how to make a contribution to the world. Pierce spoke about the way relationship comes first in his classrooms, and how each small interaction between teacher and student shapes whether a young person will give or hold back. They reflected on rigor, relevance, and relationships, on visual and auditory learners, on the early connections of Acadia rusticators to Harvard, and on the place of innovative leaders inside Maine schools and colleges who help students find their own particular fit.
