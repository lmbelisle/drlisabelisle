Poetry & Public Art #286

"Imagine each day you get to start again and again. How many days does the janitor enter the room of your soul, wipe it clean..." — Stuart Kestenbaum, *A Cold Rain*

Episode summary

Maine Poet Laureate Stuart Kestenbaum and Donna McNeil, former director of the Maine Arts Commission, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about poetry, public art, and the Maine Live gathering of creative Mainers. Kestenbaum, then interim president of Maine College of Art and the author of four collections of poems including Only Now, reflected on the rare quiet of a place like Haystack Mountain School of Crafts on Deer Isle, where he had served as director for twenty-seven years. McNeil described the moment she sold her farmhouse and her business in another state, moved to Maine without a clear plan, and built a career in arts leadership. From poetry and craft education to public art commissions, arts policy, and the conviction that creative life belongs in the public square, the conversation considered the place of artists in Maine civic life in a small state with a long literary memory.
