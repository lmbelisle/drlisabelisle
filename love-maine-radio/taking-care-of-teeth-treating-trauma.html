Taking Care of Teeth & Treating Trauma #281

Episode summary

Dr. Jon Ryder, dean of the University of New England College of Dental Medicine, and Dr. Gregory Nevens, a health psychologist and integrative practitioner, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about the next generation of Maine healers. Ryder reflected on the long-held interest in service that drew him toward underserved communities, and on the work of preparing the dental school's first graduating class for practice in 2017. Nevens described emerging approaches to repatterning the brain and nervous system in cases of trauma and chronic pain, and the methodological puzzles of researching integrative medicine in a clinical setting where the act of observation can change what is observed. From dental education and rural access to care to trauma-informed practice, mind-body medicine, and the future of the Maine health workforce, the conversation considered what it takes to train clinicians for the work the state needs in a small state with a growing health care sector.
