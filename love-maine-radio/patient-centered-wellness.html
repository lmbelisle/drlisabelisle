Episode summary

Dr. Ralph Thieme, a Falmouth osteopathic physician with training in acupuncture and a part of the integrative medicine program in family medicine at Maine Medical Center, Dr. Richard Maurer, and Maurer's longtime patient and writer Patty Hagge joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about patient-centered wellness. Thieme described the physician's role as first and foremost a teacher, not a prescriber or diagnostician, and the relief on a patient's face when understanding arrives. Maurer reflected on putting the power back in the person who needs to make diet and fitness changes, and on doctors as facilitators of partnership rather than authority. Hagge described what it feels like to be in control of her own care, to read her own blood work, and to tweak the plan with her doctor as life changes. The conversation considered acupuncture, osteopathic manipulation, naturopathic medicine, and the healing traditions that inform integrative care.
