Episode summary

Akari co-founder Dr. Ed Jaccoma, Avena Botanicals founder Deb Soule, and Hardy Girls Healthy Women's Anne Belden joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about the body beautiful. Dr. Jaccoma, working in the world of cosmetic and integrative care, made the case that the external is the smaller part of the whole, and that what is happening inside the body shows up on the outside. Soule reflected on the work of helping healthcare providers and ordinary people reintegrate common sense and a felt connection to the earth, the larger connectedness that sustains us beyond what pills out of a bottle can do. Belden, of Hardy Girls Healthy Women, spoke about resilience and self-worth in girls and the cultural pressures that work against both. With co-host Genevieve Morgan, Dr. Belisle examined beauty as something far deeper than skin, and a University of New England segment surfaced research on the body-image pressures that men also quietly carry.
