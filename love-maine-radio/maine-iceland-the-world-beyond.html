Episode summary

Petur Peterson, Portland station manager for the Icelandic shipping company Eimskip, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio along with Janine Bisaillon-Cary, president and state director of the Maine International Trade Center, and Dana Eidsness, director of the Maine North Atlantic Development Office. Peterson described Eimskip, founded in 1914 as the Icelandic Steamship Company with participation from roughly twenty percent of the country's population, and the containers now moving inbound goods like German chocolate and outbound Maine blueberries through Portland's waterfront. Bisaillon-Cary and Eidsness spoke about the work of opening Maine to international trade and to the wider North Atlantic, and the steady relationships their offices build with partners abroad. The conversation moved across the Portland working waterfront, Iceland's history of self-determination through shipping, the cultural exchange that grows alongside trade routes, and the economic doors that open when a small state and a small country choose to look outward together across the North Atlantic.
