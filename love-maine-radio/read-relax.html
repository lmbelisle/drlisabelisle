Episode summary

Josh Christie, co-owner of Print: A Bookstore, and James and Amy Harder, founders of Float Harder Relaxation Center, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about two new Portland businesses devoted to rejuvenation. Christie, the author of four books including Skiing Maine, described the work of opening an independent bookstore on Portland's East End and his deep appreciation for every author whose book lands on the shelves, and for the years of work behind each one. The Harders reflected on introducing flotation therapy to a community that had not heard of it, and on the patience required to build a practice around stillness. From independent bookselling and Maine writing communities to flotation, sensory deprivation, stress recovery, and the small daily rituals that help people slow down, the conversation considered what it takes to make space for rest in a busy season in a Portland winter in the holiday season.
