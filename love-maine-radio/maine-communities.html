Episode summary

Eleanor Kinney, philanthropist and co-founder of No Small Potatoes, and Meredith Jones, president and CEO of the Maine Community Foundation, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about Maine communities and the giving spirit that runs through them. Kinney described food as the integrator for the issues she cares about, from open space conservation to rural economic development, and the work of No Small Potatoes, an investment club providing microloans to Maine farmers and food businesses. She spoke about the slow money movement and Maine's especially active chapter. Jones reflected on the unique scale of Maine communities, big enough to enjoy and small enough to know one's neighbors. The conversation moved through philanthropy, the Maine Farmland Trust, the Environmental Funders Network, local agriculture, and the place of community foundations in shaping the health of a state during a season of giving. Together they considered what makes a Mainer show up for a neighbor.
