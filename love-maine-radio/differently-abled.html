Episode summary

Scott Wentzell, marketing director at Thos. Moser Cabinetmakers, and Charlie McBrady of Zachau Construction, both members of the Morrison Center board of directors, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about raising differently abled children and what they bring to the people around them. Wentzell, whose son Scotty has the rare Dubowitz syndrome and recently turned thirteen, spoke about the way Scotty teaches the people he meets and shines a light on what children with differences can offer rather than what they cannot. McBrady reflected that his family's life is the only one he has ever known, that everyone has bumps in the road, and that the people around them carry them through what comes. The conversation moved through parenting, neighborhood, community, the steady support of family and friends, and the work of the Morrison Center, and looked closely at the gifts that can come from a life lived outside the usual frame.
