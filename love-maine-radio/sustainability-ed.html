Episode summary

Jay Friedlander, founder of the Sustainable Business Program at College of the Atlantic and the inaugural Sharpe-McNally Chair of Green and Socially Responsible Business, and University of Maine School of Law Professor Sarah Schindler joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about what sustainability looks like in practice. Friedlander described a curriculum centered on how social, economic, and environmental capital, taken together, spark innovation and competitive advantage rather than competing with it. He spoke about the quiet winters and full summers of life in Bar Harbor and the three-hour drive between the two coasts of his Maine. Schindler brought a legal lens to sustainability questions, considering the policy structures that shape what is possible. The conversation moved across business education, law and land use, the practical work of educating tomorrow's leaders, and the way Maine campuses are shaping a generation prepared to put sustainability into practice inside real organizations and real communities.
