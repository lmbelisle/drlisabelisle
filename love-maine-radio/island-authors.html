Island Authors #297

"I don't write for money. I don't fish for money. It's not about numbers of books sold. It's not about pounds of fish caught. It's something more. And I know what it is with fishing. I haven't discovered what it is with writing yet." — Linda Greenlaw

Episode summary

Best selling author Linda Greenlaw and novelist Jane Goodrich joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about island life, writing, and the pull of the Maine coast. Greenlaw, a native of Isle au Haut and the only female swordfish captain of her generation, reflected on the strange path from fishing to publishing, the surprise of being paid in advance for a book, and her new novel Shiver Hitch. Goodrich, author of The House at Lobster Cove, described the long arc of researching and recreating an architecturally significant home on Swan's Island, and the questions readers and agents have begun asking about what she might write next. The conversation moved between rugged geography, family reading lives, and the way Maine's islands keep shaping the stories their writers tell. Both guests returned to ideas of place, persistence, and the patience writing requires, and reflected on how an island setting can keep returning a writer to the same themes across a career, as it did for Greenlaw on Isle au Haut and for Goodrich on Swan's Island.
