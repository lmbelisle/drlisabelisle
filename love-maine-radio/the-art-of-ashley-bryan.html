Episode summary

Ashley Bryan, the celebrated artist, illustrator, and author of children's books including Beautiful Blackbird and Freedom Over Me, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a special episode recorded at his home on Little Cranberry Island. Bryan, then ninety-three, described his early years in the Bronx, his service with a battalion of stevedores on the beach at Normandy, and his postwar request to attend the Glasgow School of Art. He reflected on a lifetime of collecting and responding to objects that carried evidence of human ingenuity, and on the way his island house had become a record of that long conversation with the makers of the world. From children's book illustration and African American storytelling to wartime memory, island life, and a long, generous career, the conversation offered a rare visit with one of Maine's most beloved artists in the place he called home on a small Maine island.
