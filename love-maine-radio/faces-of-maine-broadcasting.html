Episode summary

Veteran news anchor Pat Callaghan of WCSH 6 News Center and meteorologist Keith Carson joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about the daily work of telling Mainers what is happening. Callaghan, who began at the station in December 1979, described what it was like to anchor live coverage of the Challenger explosion before he had even seen the video, and the way preparation, restraint, and the ability to speak extemporaneously shape a career in news. Carson reflected on the weather that captured him as a child in New England, and on his time at The Weather Channel covering tornadoes and severe storms before returning home to forecast. From breaking news and broadcast craft to weather science, public trust, and the rhythms of a New England news cycle, the conversation considered what it takes to be a steady voice on the air in a Maine living room.
