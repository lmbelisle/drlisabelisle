Episode summary

Billy Shore, founder and CEO of Share Our Strength, and Sam Hayward, chef and co-owner of Fore Street in Portland, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about trailblazing, hunger, and the long roads that bring people to meaningful work. Shore traced the founding of Share Our Strength in 1984, born from his response to famine in Ethiopia, and his ongoing commitment to ending childhood hunger in America. He reflected on the place of failure and misstep in shaping a life of public purpose. Hayward, an early Maine supporter of Share Our Strength, spoke about the inequality of access to good food and the slow, communal work of making local food available to everyone in the state. Together they considered authentic lives, the value of taking many paths at once, the lessons of Robert Frost's road not taken, and the way Maine has become a setting where people can build something that matters.
