Episode summary

Christy Hemenway of Gold Star Honeybees and author of The Thinking Beekeeper, and Dr. Theo Cherbuliez, psychiatrist and apitherapist, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio for a conversation about bees and what they have to teach. Hemenway, a TEDxDirigo speaker, described the Top Bar Hive system she designed to let bees build their own natural beeswax honeycomb, and the warning of colony collapse disorder, where pesticides, soil, air, and water all show how interconnected life is. She quoted John Muir on the impossibility of tugging on one thing in nature without finding it attached to everything else. Cherbuliez spoke about apitherapy, including the careful use of bee venom in treatment, and the way patients learn to identify where and how many stings they need on a given day. The conversation considered pollination, local honey for allergies, urban and suburban gardening, and the long history of human beings learning from these insects.
