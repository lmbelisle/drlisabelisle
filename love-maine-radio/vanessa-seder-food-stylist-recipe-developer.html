LOVE MAINE RADIO · DECEMBER 8, 2017

Vanessa Seder, Food Stylist & Recipe Developer

Episode summary

Vanessa Seder, a food stylist and recipe developer, joined Dr. Lisa Belisle on Love Maine Radio to discuss the work of building beautiful, edible images and developing recipes that find their way into cookbooks, magazines, and home kitchens. The conversation traced the craft of food styling, the practical decisions that shaped a photograph of food before a camera ever opened its shutter, and the parallel work of recipe development, in which a dish had to taste right, behave consistently in the kitchen, and read clearly on the page for a home cook. Seder talked about the rhythm of working with editors, photographers, and clients, the influences that shaped her palette as a cook and a stylist, and the texture of a creative life built around food in Maine. The conversation moved through cooking, design, the styling of food for print and screen, and the patient hand work that brought a finished recipe to a reader's table.
