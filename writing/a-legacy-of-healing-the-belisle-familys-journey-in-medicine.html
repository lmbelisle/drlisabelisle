A Legacy of Healing: The Belisle Family's Journey in Medicine

Published in the UVM Larner Med Blog, June 6, 2025 — a family legacy piece tracing three generations of Belisle physicians through the University of Vermont College of Medicine.

See published URL. Drafted in Claude.ai with multiple editorial rounds. Key facts confirmed in the piece: Charlie MD UVM 1971; Lisa MD UVM 1996; Amy and Adelle UVM MD graduates; Campbell now a physician (third generation); father died July 8 2024; first great-grandchild born March 18, 2025. Byline: Lisa Belisle, MD, PhD, MBA, MPH, FAAFP, CPE.
